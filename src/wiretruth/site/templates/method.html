Method

Recorded provider responses are replayed through each library. Requirements compare the library's observation with the fixture's truth.

Surfaces

A primary surface is the library's normal result; a raw surface is provider metadata available outside that result. An absent surface means the information is unavailable; requirements decide whether a raw or absent value changes the grade.

Fixture tiers

recorded
Provider response bytes captured from a real request, with recording provenance.
documented
A response transcribed from provider documentation, with a source link.
derived
A named transform of a source fixture, with a rationale and evidence.

Limitations

Recorded responses replay one moment of a provider. Documented fixtures are transcribed from documentation. A grade covers one library version and the call the shim makes.

Disclosure

Findings and upstream status are tracked in upstream.json, with issue and PR links when present. Some entries cite documented behavior rather than an upstream issue.
